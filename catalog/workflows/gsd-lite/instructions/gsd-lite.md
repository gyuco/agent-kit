## Workflow: GSD lite
State lives in `.planning/` (PROJECT, ROADMAP, STATE, phases/NN-*/).
`gsd-new-project` once, then per phase: `gsd-discuss-phase` → `gsd-plan-phase` → `gsd-execute-phase` → `gsd-verify-phase`.
Keep context small: each plan is executed fresh from its file, never from chat history.
